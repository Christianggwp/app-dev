# My Favorite Movie

## Forrest Gump (1994)

**Genre:** Drama, Romance, Comedy

**Release Year:** 1994

### About the Movie

Forrest Gump is a movie about a simple
man named Forrest who experiences many
unexpected events in his life. Despite
his challenges, he continues to move
forward and never gives up.

### Why I Like It

- It has an inspiring story.
- It teaches the importance of never giving up.
- It shows the value of friendship and love.
- It has many memorable moments.

### My Favorite Character

**Forrest Gump**

I like Forrest because he is kind,
honest, and determined. He teaches us
that we should always keep moving
forward despite life's challenges.
